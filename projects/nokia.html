Internship · Optical communications

Optical test engineering at Nokia

Optical characterization, laboratory automation and measurement analysis for optical communications hardware.

What Nokia’s optical team does

Nokia builds optical networking systems that carry large volumes of data over fiber for telecom networks and data center connections. These systems carry multiple channels on different wavelengths of light. Wavelength-selective switches help direct those channels where they need to go. Nokia’s optical networking overview

How I assisted

  • Built, calibrated, and troubleshot lab setups for testing wavelength-selective switches using tunable lasers, optical spectrum analyzers, and power meters.
  • Migrated MATLAB test routines to Python to automate instrument control, data collection, and measurement processing.
  • Improved measurement quality by detecting detector saturation, automating attenuation adjustments, and filtering noise from oscilloscope traces.
  • Automated parts of test reporting so engineers could review results alongside the measurements that supported them.
